About Us

About Digital Seva

Digital Seva is a dedicated online application assistance and facilitation initiative based in Karnataka. We assist citizens in navigating complex digital portals, understanding required documentation, and submitting accurate online applications for essential civic services.

From PAN cards and voter registrations to passport guidance, income certificates, and scholarship portals, our mission is to eliminate confusion and make digital governance accessible to every applicant.

We respect your time and confidentiality. Whether you prefer to upload files online or communicate directly over WhatsApp, our objective is to provide attentive, courteous, and dependable service.
